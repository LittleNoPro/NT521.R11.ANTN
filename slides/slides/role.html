CVE-GENIE takes a CVE ID, gathers the repo and advisory, rebuilds the vulnerable environment, synthesizes an exploit, and generates a verifier that confirms it — producing reproducible instances at scale. The headline results: about 51% reproduced at an average of $2.77 each.
