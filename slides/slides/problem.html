new CVEs disclosed per year (NVD) — far beyond manual triage

Databases like NVD mostly alert administrators — they rarely ship a working exploit or a verification step. Reproduction has to be automated to scale.

NT521.R11.ANTN · Reproducing CVE-GENIE

The motivation: ML-based vulnerability detection and patching need high-quality datasets with trustworthy ground truth. Producing them by hand does not scale — Mu et al. spent over 3,600 expert-hours on 368 bugs. CVE-GENIE automates this.
